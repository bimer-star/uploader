Uploader

Uploader is a small, self-hosted personal tool that uploads and schedules videos to the owner's own YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API.

How it works:

  • The owner connects their own YouTube channel once, using Google sign-in.
  • The owner prepares videos and upload schedules in the tool's own control panel.
  • The tool then uploads the videos to the connected channel on the configured schedule.

The tool is for the owner's personal use only. It does not collect, sell, or share personal data with third parties. Google account access is used only to upload and manage videos on channels the owner has connected.
