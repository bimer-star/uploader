Privacy Policy — Uploader

Effective date: 16 November 2026.

What this app is

Uploader is a self-hosted personal tool that uploads and schedules videos to the owner's own YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, the app receives access through Google sign-in to:

  • basic Google account information (name and email address), and
  • the connected YouTube channel and its videos, only as needed to upload, schedule, and manage videos.

How data is used

Accessed data is used only to perform the upload and scheduling tasks the owner configures. The app does not use the data for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and channel data are stored on the owner's own server. Data is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party.

Your control

The owner can disconnect a channel at any time from the app's control panel or by revoking the app's access in their Google account settings. To request deletion of stored data, contact siominote913@gmail.com.
